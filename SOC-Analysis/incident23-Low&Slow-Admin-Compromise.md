Incident 23- Low and Slow Admin Compromise
Suspicious IP:
192.168.1.10
Attack Type:

Low and Slow Brute Force

Findings:

Access to login page
Low-frequency failed logins
Successful admin authentication
Access to dashboard and backup

Timeline:

Login page access
Low-frequency authentication attempts
Successful login
Access to privileged resources

MITRE ATT&CK:

Brute Force
Valid Accounts

Conclusion:
The attack used a low-and-slow approach that generated fewer indicators than a traditional brute-force attack. This behavior increases the likelihood of evading detection.
